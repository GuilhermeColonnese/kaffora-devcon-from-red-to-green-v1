About Kaffora

Kaffora is a small specialty roaster in São Paulo. We buy green coffee directly from farms we visit every harvest and roast it in 15 kg batches every Monday.

Every bag carries the farm, the variety, the process and the roast date, so you always know what is in your cup.

Our sourcing

We pay at least 40% above the commodity price and publish every contract. Our partner farms are in Minas Gerais, Huila and the Gedeo zone of Ethiopia.

Packaging is compostable, and shipping in Brazil is carbon-offset.
